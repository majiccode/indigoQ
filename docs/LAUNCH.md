## Short announcement

I’ve been building **indigoQ**, a free, open-source visual workbench that runs
locally on your computer.

Connect small workspaces to read PDFs aloud, explore data, use local AI, design
circuits, and compose 3D scenes. **v0.1.6 is out**, with a new Creative Workshop
starter canvas. Portable downloads are available for macOS, Windows, and Linux.

Try one connection: **PDF → Document Preview → Text to Speech**. Choose a voice
and click **Speak it all**—no Ollama model required.

I’d love to hear which workflow you try and where you get stuck.

**Title:** Show HN: indigoQ – A local visual workbench for documents, data and AI

**URL:** https://majiccode.github.io/indigoQ/

**First comment:**

Hi HN, I’m Paul, the developer of indigoQ. It’s an open-source, local-first app
where you connect small workspaces on a canvas and keep each step visible.

A simple workflow is FileWorkspace → Document Preview → Text to Speech: add a
PDF with extractable text, select it, and listen through browser speech
synthesis. Another is CSV → Data Table → Chart Studio → exported chart. Ollama
is optional for local AI workflows.

The new release also includes circuit editing and connectivity checks, a 3D
modeler/scene composer, ASCII/Braille conversion, mood controls, and WebUSB
debugging. Creative Workshop is a ready-wired example to explore these tools.
Circuit exports are planning notes, not fabrication-ready PCB files.

Portable bundles include the app runtime for macOS, Windows, and Linux. It’s
built with Next.js/React and a FastAPI backend. I’d especially appreciate
feedback on installation, the first-run experience, and which connections feel
useful.

## 45-second PDF read-aloud recording script

Use a short PDF you created or have permission to share, with selectable text.
This is a recording plan; no demo video has been recorded yet.

| Time | Screen action | Caption / narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 s | Show the canvas with three workspaces | “What if you could listen to this PDF?” |
| 5–12 s | Drop the PDF into FileWorkspace | “Add a document to your local workbench.” |
| 12–22 s | Connect FileWorkspace → Document Preview and select the PDF | “Preview the document and extract its text.” |
| 22–32 s | Connect Document Preview → Text to Speech | “Connect the text to a voice.” |
| 32–40 s | Choose a voice, click Speak it all, let a sentence play | “Read aloud. No Ollama model needed.” |
| 40–45 s | Show the canvas and website address | “indigoQ. One canvas for your ideas. Free and open source.” |

Keep captions visible so the recording is understandable without sound. Use the
actual app screen and speech playback; keep documents and account details out
of frame unless intended for the demo.
